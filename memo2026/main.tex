\section{Purpose}

the 2026 sidecar analysis results and the updated rollout spreadsheet in
\texttt{WSU SDD data volume.xlsx} (\texttt{rollout v2} worksheet).  It is
intended as a concise numerical update rather than a full replacement for the
existing methodology memos.

Notes carried in the spreadsheet:
\begin{itemize}
\item MS1 assumes 6\,s and 47 antennas; current CASA+pipeline implies that the
  original SoC methodology is not directly applicable, so scaling from the
  current cluster is recommended.
\item MS4 and MS5 assume 3\,s integration time.
\item The FLOPS-based system-performance estimate should be divided by 2
  (4k hr versus 8.7k hr) and multiplied by 3 as a safety factor before
  converting to cores, as in the Compute TSI and SDD.
\item The max-data-rate line uses the IST value for the 12\,m MS5 peak to give a
  consistent top-line total of 4.434 GB/s.
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary}
The 2026 sidecar analysis continues to support a substantial reduction relative
to the original uniform-finest-resolution extrapolation when the current SPW
resolution distribution is preserved.  The rollout-v2 spreadsheet then applies
additional operational and product-side mitigations to reach the updated top-line
data-volume, rate, and compute numbers summarized above.
