Key files:

*inputs.md - manually collected high-level results from the current `ryanCy11`
 sidecar analyses, including current-data volume fractions, setup-summary
 context, and sample-driven WSU projection factors.

*WSU SDD data volume.xlsx - spreadsheet of updated rollout numbers.  The
 `rollout v2` worksheet is the primary source for the draft memo tables.

*main.tex - concise LaTeX memo documenting the updated projections.

*main.pdf - compiled PDF of the memo.

*setup_summary_appendix/ - generated appendix assets for the memo's spectral
 setup summary, including machine-readable tables and the bandwidth /
 resolution histogram figures.
